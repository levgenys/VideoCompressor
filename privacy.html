Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Shrinka compresses videos on your device. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, so there is little to tell. Here is all of it.

In short

  • Videos are processed on your device and are never uploaded.
  • We do not collect personal data. The app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics, for example that a compression started or the subscription screen was shown; it never sends your videos, file names, locations or contact details.
  • The only network requests the app makes are App Store purchases, syncing the free compression counter through your iCloud, and the anonymous usage statistics described below. Videos stored in iCloud Photos are downloaded by iOS itself before compression.

Access to Photos

Shrinka asks for access to your Photos library to show your videos, compress the ones you pick and save the result. You can open the whole library or only selected videos. The app reads videos and their details (size, duration, resolution, date, albums) on the device only: to show them to you and to work out the compression options.

The compressed copy is saved to your library with the original's date, in its albums and in the “Shrinka” album. The location and camera details recorded in the original are carried over with it. The app deletes an original only at your command and after the iOS system confirmation.

During compression a temporary file is created in the app's storage; it is removed as soon as the result is saved.

When you send a video to Shrinka through the Share menu, the extension tells the app on the same device only the file name and size, so the app can find that video in the library.

Free version and purchases

Free compressions are counted on your device. The counter is kept on the device and in the iCloud of your Apple Account, so it is shared by your devices. It contains nothing about you or your videos, and we have no access to it.

Shrinka Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and the subscription; we never see your payment details, name or address. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To understand which parts of the app are used, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics. It counts events such as opening the app, starting, finishing or cancelling a compression, choosing a sort order or a filter, showing the subscription screen or making a purchase. Some events carry numbers: how many videos were in the queue and how many of them were compressed, which threshold is set in the large videos filter. These events never contain your videos, frames from them, file names, dates or locations.

Firebase assigns each installation of the app a random identifier and records the device model, iOS version, country, language and app version. This identifier is not linked to your name, Apple Account or email address; it is reset when the app is deleted. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Google Privacy Policy.

The app contains no crash reporting library. If you have allowed iOS to share analytics with developers, Apple may pass us anonymized crash reports through its own system. You can change this in iOS Settings: Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

When you write to us

If you send a problem report or a suggestion from the app, your mail app opens with a pre-filled message. The end of the message lists the app version, device model and iOS version: this helps us reproduce problems. You can delete that line before sending. We use your address and message only to reply and share them with no one.

Deleting data

Shrinka keeps no videos of its own: both originals and compressed copies live in your Photos library and are deleted there. Deleting the app erases its settings and temporary files. The free compression counter contains nothing about you or your videos.

Children

Shrinka collects no personal data and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with data, it will be described on this page and the date at the top will change. By continuing to use the app after a change you accept the updated policy.
