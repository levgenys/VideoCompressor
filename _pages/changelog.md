### `Latest`

## 1.0
**Aug, 2025**

Shrinka — Compress videos, free up space, and keep your memories
Videos take up tons of storage, but deleting them feels wrong? Shrinka helps you reduce video size by up to 10× — with no visible quality loss. Free up space and keep what matters.

Perfect for those who:
	• Shoot in 4K and constantly run out of space
	• Moved their video archive to a hard drive, but still want to keep copies on their phone
	• Want to share videos without wasting mobile data
	• Don’t want to delete precious memories just to save space

Two modes — simple or advanced:
	• Simple mode — just one tap and your video is compressed
	• Advanced mode — customize resolution, frame rate, and bitrate.
Shrinka even shows the estimated file size before compression.

100% local and safe

Your privacy comes first:
	• Videos never leave your device
	• Compression happens entirely offline
	• No accounts, no cloud, no data tracking
	• The original video stays intact — unless you choose to remove it

Reduce file size by up to 10×

Especially powerful with 4K videos. Your videos will look nearly the same, but take up way less space.

With Shrinka, you can:
	• Keep more videos on your phone
	• Back up your originals and save lightweight copies for everyday use
	• Share videos faster — without losing quality or hitting size limits

Shrinka — simple, secure, and offline.
Keep your memories. Free your storage. Take control — effortlessly.
